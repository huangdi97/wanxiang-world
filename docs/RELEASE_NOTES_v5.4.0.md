# Wanxiang v5.4.0 Stable Release Notes

## Verified scope

v5.4.0 covers the generalized Source → Living World product chain across:

- the requalified first real Chinese TXT book;
- the accepted second real EPUB book;
- the accepted public historical GEDCOM Family World path;
- structured JSON/CSV and mixed-source regression paths;
- Worldness adversarial calibration, package/replay/branch, rights, privacy,
  and security gates.

The first-book acceptance evidence is
`reports/M84_FIRST_BOOK_REQUALIFICATION.md`; the original pre-repair
`NOT_ACCEPTED` report remains preserved as historical evidence.

## Product boundaries

The system does not guarantee arbitrary Source inputs are 100% automatically
correct. Semantic providers are optional and replaceable; their outputs are
provenance-bound Candidates/Proposals, not Canonical World State. Evidence,
review, uncertainty, conflict, and rights gates remain part of the product
contract.

## Rights and privacy

Private sources remain outside Git and are not sent to external models or used
for training by the qualified local paths. Public historical Family E2E is real
product validation, but private living-family user validation was not performed.
Living-person consent, viewer rights, biometric, voice-like, posthumous, and
public-export boundaries remain enforced by the existing privacy/security
contracts.

## Out of scope

No model training, v5.5 work, arbitrary-source correctness claim, or deployment
to external infrastructure is included in this release.
